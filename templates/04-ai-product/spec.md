# 04 · AI Product Manager, build spec

- **Font:** Calibri (fallback Arial).
- **Sizes:** Name 18pt bold, headings 12pt bold, body 10.5pt.
- **Signature element, "AI Product Highlights":** one line under the summary naming shipped AI products + the eval/latency/cost story, plain bold text.
- **Skills:** lead with the AI stack (LLMs, RAG, evals, prompt design, vector DBs) then data + product.
- **Bullets:** foreground model evals, quality vs latency vs cost tradeoffs, AI-feature adoption, human-in-the-loop/guardrails.
- **Sections:** Summary · AI Product Highlights · Experience · Skills (AI + data first) · Education · Certifications.
- **Margins:** 0.7 in, single column, US Letter. ATS-safe.
