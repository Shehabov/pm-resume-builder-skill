# 02 · Modern Minimal, build spec

- **Font:** Calibri (fallback Arial). Clean sans.
- **Sizes:** Name 18pt bold, Title 11.5pt, section headings 12pt bold, body 10.5pt, tech/skills 10pt.
- **Margins:** 0.7 in. **Line spacing:** 1.12. Single column, US Letter.
- **Header:** Name left or centered; Title next line; contact line with pipes.
- **Section headings:** Title Case, bold, thin bottom rule.
- **Experience:** Company + location line; Title + dates line (right-aligned dates with a hyphen, "Present"); one context line; 5-7 / 3-5 outcome bullets.
- **Sections:** Summary · Experience · Skills (2-3 grouped lines) · Education · Certifications (optional).
- **ATS:** single column, standard headings, no graphics.
