# 03 · Growth / Metrics-Forward, build spec

- **Font:** Calibri (fallback Arial).
- **Sizes:** Name 18pt bold, headings 12pt bold, body 10.5pt.
- **Signature element, "Selected Impact" strip:** directly under the summary, one bold line of 3-5 headline metrics separated by " · " (plain text, NO chart/graphic): e.g. **Activation +18% · $4M revenue · 6M users · Retention +12%**.
- **Skills:** lead with data/experimentation (SQL, Amplitude, A/B testing) then product.
- **Sections:** Summary · Selected Impact · Experience (metric-led bullets) · Skills · Education.
- **Margins:** 0.7 in, single column, US Letter. ATS: metrics are plain bold text, not images.
