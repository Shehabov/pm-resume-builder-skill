# 01 · Executive Serif, build spec

- **Font:** EB Garamond (fallback Garamond, then Georgia). ATS-safe serif.
- **Sizes:** Name 20pt bold, Title 12pt bold, section headings 12.5pt bold small-caps, body 10.5pt, dates/location 10.5pt.
- **Margins:** 0.6 in. **Line spacing:** 1.12. **Page:** US Letter, single column.
- **Header:** Name centered; Title centered under it; location centered; contact line centered with pipe separators.
- **Section headings:** UPPERCASE, bold, with a full-width thin bottom rule (0.5pt black).
- **Experience:** Company (bold) left + City,Country (bold) right on line 1; Title (bold) left + Dates (bold) right on line 2; a one-line italic/regular company-context paragraph; then bullets (•), 5-7 recent / 3-5 older, no trailing periods, dates as "Aug 2024 - Present".
- **Sections:** Summary · Professional Experience · Product Advisory & Mentorship (optional) · Education · Product Training & Certifications · Awards.
- **ATS:** no tables-for-layout (use tab stops for right alignment), no icons, no columns.
