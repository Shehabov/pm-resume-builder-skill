# PM Bullet Writing Guide

**Sources:** IGotAnOffer (13 PM resume examples), Exponent (FAANG PM resume guide), HustleBadger (write a PM resume), Harvard Career Services, Google XYZ formula, Columbia STAR, Lenny Rachitsky / Shreyas Doshi product-thinking principles.

A recruiter reads a PM bullet in about one second. It has to carry a result on its own.

---

## The PM bullet formula

**`[Action verb] + [what you shipped or owned] + [for whom / scope / domain] + [the measurable outcome]`**

Push the metric toward the front when you have a strong one ("Cut checkout drop-off 22% by…"). This is the "skill: accomplishment" / TAR (Task-Action-Result) structure every top PM guide converges on.

### The transformation that matters most

| ❌ Duty (weak) | ✅ Outcome (strong) |
|---|---|
| Responsible for the mobile checkout experience and working with engineering | Cut mobile checkout drop-off 22% by shipping a one-tap flow with eng and design, adding ~$1.4M in annual revenue |
| Worked cross-functionally to launch new features | Aligned eng, design, and 3 CSM teams to redesign the campaign builder, cutting setup from 40 to under 10 minutes and lifting first-month activation 10% |
| Managed the product roadmap | Owned the 12-month roadmap across 5 product lines, sequencing bets by revenue impact and cutting time-to-market 30% |
| Conducted user research | Ran 60+ discovery interviews with CHROs and L&D leaders; insights reshaped the roadmap and the Use-Cases system |
| Helped grow the product | Grew activation 18% via a self-serve onboarding redesign, validated through 4 A/B tests |

The pattern: **remove the duty framing, name the surface you owned, name who it was for, and land a number.**

## Show the PM arc: strategy, execution, impact

Recruiters read a PM through three lenses. Cover all three across a role's bullets.

- **Strategy / product sense**, what problem, what bet, what tradeoff, what success metric you defined and owned. *Senior resumes lead with these.*
- **Execution / delivery**, what you actually shipped, with whom, at what scope and speed. *Early-career resumes lead with these.*
- **Impact**, the number that moved and the business result.

A strong role usually has: 1-2 strategy/ownership bullets, 2-3 execution bullets, and each one lands an outcome.

### The one-line company context (PM "domain context")

Under each role title, add ONE line describing the company/product, its scale, and what you owned. This is the single biggest differentiator on a PM resume.

> **Lead Product Manager**, Lumofy, Riyadh, SA, Aug 2024 - Present
> *AI-powered talent-development SaaS ($40M valuation, 500+ MENA enterprises). Led 5 core product lines end-to-end and headed the product department.*

Without it, "Launched Performance Management 2.0" is a mystery. With it, the recruiter instantly understands the stakes.

## Action verbs for PMs

**Strategy & ownership:** Owned, Defined, Set, Drove, Shaped, Prioritized, Sequenced, Bet on, Repositioned, Championed, Aligned

**Discovery & insight:** Ran (discovery), Interviewed, Uncovered, Diagnosed, Validated, Synthesized, Identified, Sized

**Execution & delivery:** Launched, Shipped, Delivered, Built, Rolled out, Scaled, Integrated, Rebuilt, Consolidated, Automated, Migrated

**Growth & optimization:** Grew, Increased, Lifted, Improved, Reduced, Cut, Accelerated, Converted, Retained, Monetized, Unlocked

**Leadership & influence:** Led, Headed, Coordinated, Mentored, Partnered, Rallied, Unified, Coached, Enabled

**Avoid the vague fillers:** Responsible for, Worked on, Helped, Assisted, Handled, Involved in, Participated in, Utilized, Leveraged (as filler), Spearheaded/Orchestrated (overused). Also cut hollow adjectives: results-driven, dynamic, passionate, strategic thinker, ninja, guru, rockstar.

## Quantify like a PM

Use digits, show the baseline or scale, and tie to business value.

**When you have hard numbers:** "$600K new Tier-1 revenue", "80% enterprise adoption", "3× DAU", "activation +18%", "time-to-market −30%", "$133K/mo saved", "14M users".

**When you do not have exact numbers, use honest scale + framing:**
- Reach: "used by 13M+ people", "tens of thousands of businesses"
- Scope: "5 core product lines", "60+ interviews", "30+ stakeholders"
- Team: "led 2 designers and 9 engineers", "cross-functional team of 6"
- Speed: "setup from 40 to under 10 minutes", "cut time-to-market 30%"
- Prefix estimates with "~" and keep them defensible in an interview.

**What counts as impact for a PM** (not only %): revenue/ARR/GMV, activation, retention/churn, conversion, DAU/MAU, adoption, CAC/LTV, NPS/CSAT, time-to-market, cost saved, users reached, deals influenced, launches delivered. See `pm-metrics-and-impact.md` for which to feature by archetype.

## Bullet mechanics (ATS + readability)

- 1-2 lines each; split anything over 3 lines into two bullets.
- No personal pronouns (I, we, my, our). Past tense for past roles; present acceptable for the current role (be consistent).
- **Do not end bullets with periods.**
- Use digits, not spelled-out numbers (8, not eight; 18%, not eighteen percent).
- Order bullets most-impressive-first within each role.
- Bullets per role: **5-7** for the two most recent, **3-5** for the next, **2-3** for old roles.
- Vary the opening verb across bullets in a role, never start three bullets the same way.
- Avoid sub-bullets, and avoid ampersands/slashes where a word reads cleaner.

---

## Never put these on a PM resume

Personal pronouns · "Responsible for" · narrative paragraphs · subjective self-praise ("excellent communicator") · obvious skills (MS Office) · salary · reasons for leaving · negative framing · internal codenames only insiders know · buzzwords without substance · a bullet with no scope and no number.
